## General facts (Swedish B-licence rules, kept general)

- Riskettan and Risktvåan are both mandatory parts of the risk education for a B licence.
- Both parts must be completed before the driving test (körprov).
- A valid körkortstillstånd is needed for practice driving and risk education.
- A licence taken in an automatic car comes with a condition that only allows driving automatic cars.
- The theory test is taken at Trafikverket.

## Not confirmed – do not state on the website

Ask the customer before writing any of these:

1. Where the Risktvåan (halkbana) is held.
2. Which languages Riskettan is taught in.
3. Whether lessons can start from Jakobsberg (pick-up) or only from the school.
4. Whether theory lessons with a teacher are given in Arabic and Spanish (homepage says lessons and theory lessons are taught in all four languages).
5. Opening days: homepage says "öppet alla dagar i veckan utom söndagar", but listed hours and Google show Monday–Friday only.
6. Test lesson length: price list says 30–50 minutes, homepage says 50 minutes.
